# Product Vision: VIDERE RettSted

## The Mission
To build the definitive operational bridge between production and economy in the logistics industry.

## The Core Concept
VIDERE RettSted began as a solution to the 'last meter' problem (visual delivery assistance). However, its architecture and roadmap position it to become a comprehensive, end-to-end operational operating system for transport and logistics companies.

It sits precisely between the moment goods are ready to be moved (Production) and the moment the job is completed and ready to be billed (Economy/ERP).

## The Gap We Fill
Historically, the logistics industry relies on fragmented systems:
1.  **TMS (Transport Management Systems):** Often rigid, legacy systems focused on high-level booking and billing, lacking real-time operational context.
2.  **Fleet Management:** Standalone GPS trackers and maintenance logs disconnected from the actual daily routes and drivers.
3.  **HR & Time Tracking:** Separate systems that don't talk to the daily operational plan, making overtime calculation and compliance a manual headache.
4.  **The Physical Reality:** Drivers using WhatsApp, paper manifests, and standard Google Maps, losing critical tribal knowledge when they leave.

**VIDERE RettSted consolidates these operational pillars into a single, cohesive, modern platform.**

## Key Differentiators
*   **Function-First & Driver-Centric:** Designed for the reality of the cab, not just the office. If the driver doesn't use it, the data is useless.
*   **API-First Extensibility:** We are not building an ERP. We are building the operational engine that *feeds* the ERP. Everything from route completion to time tracking will be accessible via API for seamless integration with accounting software.
*   **Hardware Agnostic Telematics (Future Exploration):** While modern vehicles have APIs, the landscape is fragmented. Our strategy is to explore ingesting telemetry data (mileage, fuel, diagnostics) from various OEM APIs, third-party aftermarket trackers, and potentially developing our own proprietary OBD-II/GPS hardware module to guarantee a unified data stream for older or mixed fleets.

*   **Constraint-Based Intelligence:** Moving beyond simple routing to true optimization—matching the specific physical requirements of an order to the capabilities of the fleet. The route planner actively cross-references cumulative order volume, weight, and pallet count against the specific constraints of the assigned vehicle to prevent overloading.
*   **Immutable Audit Trail:** From the moment a loader scans a package onto a truck, to the GPS-verified Proof of Delivery, to the admin approving the overtime, every action is logged, ensuring complete process integrity and GDPR compliance.

## The Ultimate Goal
To be the default operational dashboard for logistics managers. They should be able to open VIDERE RettSted and instantly know:
*   Where their vehicles are and their mechanical status.
*   Who is driving, if they are legally compliant, and their exact working hours.
*   The real-time status of every single order and route.
*   That all of this operational data is flowing automatically and correctly into their billing systems.
