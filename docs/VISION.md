# Product Vision: VIDERE RettSted

## The Mission
To build the definitive operational bridge between the terminal and the customer's door. We solve the "Last Meter" problem and eliminate loading errors.

## The Core Concept
VIDERE RettSted began as a solution to the 'last meter' problem—providing visual, precise delivery assistance where standard GPS fails. 

While the architecture is robust enough to handle comprehensive logistics, our strategic focus is to be the undisputed master of the **Physical Execution Layer**. We connect the Dispatcher, the Loader, and the Driver in a seamless, error-free real-time loop.

## The Gap We Fill
Historically, the logistics industry relies on fragmented, legacy systems (TMS, WMS, FMS, HRIS). These systems are powerful for booking and billing, but they are terrible at the actual physical execution of the job.

**The Problem:**
1.  **The "Where is the door?" Problem:** TMS systems send a text address. Drivers waste time finding the actual entrance, loading dock, or specific building.
2.  **The "Wrong Box on the Truck" Problem:** Terminal workers lack simple, integrated tools to verify that the right packages are getting on the right truck for a specific route.
3.  **The "Did they deliver it?" Problem:** Dispatchers lack real-time, visually verified Proof of Delivery (POD) linked directly to the route plan.

**VIDERE RettSted is not an "All-in-One" ERP. We are the precision execution engine that sits below the TMS and WMS.**

## Our Strategic "Wedge"
We will win by focusing intensely on the daily operational loop, ignoring the bloated features of legacy software:

1.  **Visual Places (RettSted):** Our unique superpower. A rich, visual database of exact delivery locations, entrances, and crucial driver notes that standard maps ignore.
2.  **Lightning-Fast Dispatch:** Making it incredibly fast for a dispatcher to build a route based on these visual places and assign it.
3.  **Flawless Loading (Manifests):** A dead-simple, real-time scanning interface for terminal workers to guarantee 100% accuracy when loading the truck.
4.  **Driver-Centric Execution & POD:** An app that tells the driver exactly where to go, what to drop off, and forces a structured, GPS-stamped Proof of Delivery with photos.

## Key Differentiators
*   **Execution-First & Driver-Centric:** Designed for the reality of the loading ramp and the truck cab, not just the office. If the driver and loader don't love using it, the data is useless.
*   **API-First Extensibility:** We are not building an ERP or a complex HR system. We are building the operational engine that *feeds* those systems. Everything from route completion to PODs will be accessible via API.
*   **Constraint-Based Intelligence (Pragmatic):** We will implement practical constraints—warning dispatchers if a route exceeds a vehicle's weight/volume limits or a driver's scheduled hours—without building a rigid, over-engineered automatic optimization engine that ignores real-world flexibility.
*   **Immutable Audit Trail:** From the moment a loader scans a package onto a truck, to the GPS-verified Proof of Delivery, every physical action is logged, ensuring complete process integrity.

## What We Will NOT Build (The Anti-Roadmap)
To maintain our focus and avoid becoming a bloated legacy system, we explicitly choose *not* to build:
*   Complex Tachograph & EU 561/2006 compliance analysis (leave to specialized hardware).
*   Full-scale HR Information Systems (LMS, deep contract management, payroll).
*   Deep Fleet Maintenance & Telematics hardware development (leave to OEM integrations).
*   Complex, multi-currency invoicing and freight cost calculation (leave to the ERP/TMS).

## The Ultimate Goal
To be the default operational screen open on the dispatcher's desk, the scanner in the loader's hand, and the phone on the driver's dashboard. 

They should be able to open VIDERE RettSted and instantly execute a flawless delivery loop, from planning to loading to verified drop-off.
